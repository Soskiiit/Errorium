Designed for Developers, Verified by Benchmarks

Errorium enforces strict academic guidelines: zero reliance on ready-made search engines (Elasticsearch, Solr, Lucene, rank-bm25). The inverted index, memory-mapped postings list, code tokenization, and BM25 ranking formulas are completely custom-built and continuously measured against Recall@k and MRR benchmarks.
